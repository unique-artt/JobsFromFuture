Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

What We Collect

This site does not require an account and does not collect personal information directly. We do not ask for your name, email, or resume through this website.

Cookies and Analytics

This site may use basic cookies or similar technologies for functionality and to understand general traffic patterns. We do not use cookies to build personal profiles or to serve targeted advertising on this site.

Third-Party Links

When you click "Apply Now," you leave this site and go to a third-party platform. That platform may collect your personal information, including your resume, contact details, and interview data. Their privacy policy governs how they handle your data. We encourage you to review it before submitting any information.

Your Rights

Because we do not collect personal data directly, there is no account or profile for you to access, correct, or delete on this site. If you have questions about data held by a third-party platform you reached through this site, please contact that platform directly.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page.
