Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Jobs From Future ("we," "us," or "our") operates jobsfromfuture.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account to browse job listings. We do not directly collect your name, email address, resume, or other personal information through this website.

We may collect non-personal technical information automatically, such as your browser type, device type, approximate location derived from IP address, pages visited, and referring URLs. This information is used for basic site functionality, security, and understanding general traffic patterns.

2. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This site may use essential cookies or similar technologies required for the site to function. We do not use cookies to build personal profiles or to serve targeted advertising on this site.

If we add third-party analytics tools in the future, those tools may set their own cookies. We will update this policy if that happens.

3. Third-Party Links and Applications

When you click "Apply Now," you leave Jobs From Future and are redirected to a third-party platform to complete your application. That platform may collect personal information, including your resume, contact details, and interview responses. Their privacy policy governs how they handle your data. We encourage you to review it before submitting any information.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party sites.

4. How We Use Information

Any technical information we collect is used to operate and maintain the site, prevent abuse, and understand general usage trends. We do not sell personal information.

5. Data Retention

We retain technical logs only for as long as necessary for security and operational purposes. Because we do not collect personal information directly, there is no personal data profile for us to retain.

6. Children's Privacy

This site is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Choices

Because we do not collect personal information directly, there is no account or profile for you to access, correct, or delete on this site. If you have questions about data held by a third-party platform you reached through this site, please contact that platform directly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us through the site.
