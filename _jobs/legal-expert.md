The research teams behind the best-known AI models need legal judgment they can't generate on their own. Your standards and expertise become the rubric that frontier AI is graded against.

This is a standing listing for lawyers who want AI training and evaluation work, not a specific job opening. It stays open whether or not a matching project is running this week. New projects start on short notice, so instead of posting and pulling a listing each time, one stays open per field and is drawn from when a project begins.

**What the work looks like**

Lawyers in the network are matched to projects like:

- Drafting a motion or a brief from a fact pattern, plus the reference answer a model's attempt gets graded against
- Grading model-drafted contract and discovery work: whether the provision does what it claims, whether the privilege call is right, whether the citation supports the proposition it is offered for
- Flagging where a model states the law correctly and would still lose, given the forum, the standard of review, or facts that do not carry the argument

Projects in this field have posted at **$100 to $150 an hour**, set per project by scope and depth.

**What we're looking for**

- A J.D., LL.B., or LL.M. and a current license to practice
- Professional experience in litigation, transactional work, or regulatory practice
- Clear written communication, since most of the work involves explaining your reasoning
- Comfort with ambiguous tasks, and willingness to flag unclear instructions

**Where people in this pool have worked**

The most common backgrounds, though the pool is broader than this:

- Am Law firms and litigation or transactional boutiques
- Federal and state judicial clerkships
- In-house counsel and compliance at large companies
- Federal and state enforcement, regulatory, and prosecutorial offices

The firm name matters less than whether you did the drafting and the analysis yourself, recently enough to explain why you came out where you did.

**How it works**

Apply here with your resume and confirm where you work from, then complete a short interview, about 20 minutes total.

Your license and background are verified and you're added to the legal pool. When a project needs your area of practice, matching people from the pool are invited to that specific listing. It names the rate, the hours, and the client, and any hiring decision is made there.

**What to expect**

This listing does not accept or reject candidates, so you will not get a decision on it. You'll be reached out to when a project matches your background. Depending on what clients are running, that can be within a week or several months.

Finishing the domain expert interview and joining every network you qualify for both improve your odds of being matched. You only need to sign up once to be eligible across all of them.

You will be engaged as an independent contractor. This is a fully remote role that can be completed on your own schedule. Payments are weekly based on services rendered. No H1-B or STEM OPT support is available at this time. This listing is for a role at an external company.
