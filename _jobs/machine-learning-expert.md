The research teams behind the best-known AI models need machine learning judgment they can't generate on their own. Your standards and expertise become the rubric that frontier AI is graded against.

This is a standing listing for machine learning practitioners who want AI training and evaluation work, not a specific job opening. It stays open whether or not a matching project is running this week. New projects start on short notice, so instead of posting and pulling a listing each time, one stays open per field and is drawn from when a project begins.

**What the work looks like**

Machine learning practitioners in the network are matched to projects like:

- Writing a modeling problem from a system you actually shipped, with the data, the objective, and the constraint, plus the reference answer a model's attempt gets graded against
- Grading model-written training and evaluation code: whether the loss matches the objective, whether the eval set leaks, whether the ablation supports the conclusion drawn from it
- Flagging where a model proposes an approach that would train cleanly and still fail in production, given distribution shift, label noise, or a metric that rewards the wrong behaviour

Comparable machine learning projects have posted at **$70 to $120 an hour**, set per project by scope and depth.

**What we're looking for**

- Professional experience training, evaluating, or deploying machine learning systems
- Clear written communication, since most of the work involves explaining your reasoning
- Comfort with ambiguous tasks, and willingness to flag unclear instructions

Where you trained matters less than whether you have put models in front of real users and dealt with what came back.

**How it works**

Apply here with your resume and confirm where you work from, then complete a short interview, about 20 minutes total.

Your background is verified and you're added to the machine learning pool. When a project needs machine learning expertise, matching people from the pool are invited to that specific listing. It names the rate, the hours, and the client, and any hiring decision is made there.

**What to expect**

This listing does not accept or reject candidates, so you will not get a decision on it. You'll be reached out to when a project matches your background. Depending on what clients are running, that can be within a week or several months.

Finishing the domain expert interview and joining every network you qualify for both improve your odds of being matched. You only need to sign up once to be eligible across all of them.

You will be engaged as an independent contractor. This is a fully remote role that can be completed on your own schedule. Payments are weekly based on services rendered. No H1-B or STEM OPT support is available at this time. This listing is for a role at an external company.
