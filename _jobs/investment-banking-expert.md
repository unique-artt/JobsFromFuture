The research teams behind the best-known AI models need investment banking judgment they can't generate on their own. Your standards and expertise become the rubric that frontier AI is graded against.

This is a standing listing for investment bankers who want AI training and evaluation work, not a specific job opening. It stays open whether or not a matching project is running this week. New projects start on short notice, so instead of posting and pulling a listing each time, one stays open per field and is drawn from when a project begins.

**What the work looks like**

Bankers in the network are matched to projects like:

- Building a three-statement model and an LBO from a filing set, then writing the rubric a grader uses to mark a model's attempt wrong
- Reviewing model-generated comps and precedent transactions, and explaining where the multiple, the calendarization, or the peer set is off
- Grading model-drafted CIM and pitch material against what an MD would send, and writing the reasoning behind each deduction

Past rates have run **$100 to $130 an hour**, set per project by scope and depth.

**What we're looking for**

- Professional experience in financial modeling, valuation, or deal execution
- Clear written communication, since most of the work involves explaining your reasoning
- Comfort with ambiguous tasks, and willingness to flag unclear instructions

**Where people in this pool have worked**

The most common backgrounds, though the pool is broader than this:

- Bulge bracket and elite boutique advisory: M&A, leveraged finance, restructuring, ECM/DCM
- Buy-side seats built on banking training: private equity, growth equity, private credit, distressed
- Corporate development and in-house M&A

The firm name matters less than whether you built the models and wrote the materials yourself, recently enough to explain the judgment calls behind them.

**How it works**

Apply here with your resume and confirm where you work from, then take a short AI interview, about 20 minutes total.

Your background is verified and you're added to the investment banking pool. When a project needs investment banking expertise, matching people from the pool are invited to that specific listing. It names the rate, the hours, and the client, and any hiring decision is made there.

**What to expect**

This listing does not accept or reject candidates, so you will not get a decision on it. You'll be reached out to when a project matches your background. Depending on what clients are running, that can be within a week or several months.

Finishing the domain expert interview and joining every network you qualify for both improve your odds of being matched. You only need to sign up once to be eligible across all of them.

You will be engaged as an independent contractor. This is a fully remote role that can be completed on your own schedule. Payments are weekly based on services rendered. No H1-B or STEM OPT support is available at this time.
