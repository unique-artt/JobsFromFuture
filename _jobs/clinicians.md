**Location:** Remote (United States)

**About the Role**

Experienced Nurse Practitioners are being hired to help evaluate and improve cutting-edge AI systems. In this role, you'll evaluate AI-generated clinical content and workflows, leveraging your real-world expertise in diagnosing, treating, and managing patient care across acute and chronic conditions.

**Key Responsibilities**

- Evaluate AI-generated clinical assessments, treatment plans, patient education materials, and other healthcare artifacts for quality and accuracy.
- Compare AI-generated responses head-to-head based on clinical reasoning, guideline adherence, and diagnostic soundness.
- Review clinical scenarios involving diagnosis, treatment planning, medication decisions, and patient education and provide expert feedback.

**Required Qualifications**

- 4+ years of experience as a Nurse Practitioner (NP), Advanced Practice Registered Nurse (APRN), Family Nurse Practitioner (FNP), Acute Care Nurse Practitioner (ACNP), Pediatric Nurse Practitioner (PNP), or another closely related advanced practice nursing role.
- Active RN licensure with specialized graduate education in nursing, and experience diagnosing and treating acute, episodic, or chronic illness.
- Ability to commit 20+ hours per week.
- Strong written English communication skills.
