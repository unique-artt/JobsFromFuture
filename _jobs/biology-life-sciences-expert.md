**Location:** Remote (United States)

**Overview**

Senior biologists and life scientists are being sought to improve how frontier AI models reason about real life sciences research.

Your scientific judgment is the substance of this role. You will review the quality of life sciences reasoning tasks and model outputs, write the problem specifications and reference solutions that define what "correct" looks like, and design benchmarks that show whether a model is genuinely improving. A practicing specialist is wanted rather than a generalist.

This role is fully remote.

**Key Responsibilities**

- **Expert review:** Assess life sciences tasks and model outputs, catching mechanistic hand-waving, unsupported causal claims, flawed experimental design, and answers that read well but would not survive peer review.
- **Problem specs and reference solutions:** Write precise problem statements and rigorous reference solutions that reflect how life sciences research is actually done.
- **Benchmarks:** Design challenging, research-level problems and evaluation sets in your area of specialization.
- **Calibration:** Work with other researchers and experts in adjacent fields to turn tacit expert judgment into explicit, teachable criteria.

**Core Qualifications**

- **Education:** PhD in biochemistry, molecular or cell biology, genetics, immunology, neuroscience, or a closely related life science.
- **Seniority:** A faculty appointment, principal investigator, staff or senior scientist, or an equivalent senior research role at a university, academic medical center, national laboratory, or research organization.
- **Domain depth:** Genuine specialization in at least one area, for example molecular and cell biology, biochemistry and structural biology, immunology, genetics and genomics, neuroscience, or ecology and evolution.
- **Research record:** A strong peer-reviewed publication record. Recognition such as competitive fellowships or awards is a plus.
- **AI fluency:** Hands-on use of large language models in your work, and the judgment to tell a sound argument from a plausible-sounding wrong one.
- **Communication:** Excellent written communication and the ability to give precise, well-structured feedback.
- **Location:** Based in the United States.

You will be engaged as an independent contractor. This is a fully remote role that can be completed on your own schedule.
