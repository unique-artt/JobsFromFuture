**About the Role**

Experienced Project Management Specialists are being hired to help evaluate and improve cutting-edge AI systems. In this role, you'll evaluate AI-generated project management content and workflows, leveraging your real-world expertise in coordinating schedules, budgets, staffing, and stakeholder communications across technical projects.

**Key Responsibilities**

- Evaluate AI-generated project plans, status reports, stakeholder communications, and other project artifacts for quality and accuracy.
- Compare AI-generated responses head-to-head based on planning rigor, execution feasibility, and stakeholder relevance.

- 4+ years of experience as a Project Management Specialist, Project Manager, Technical Project Manager, Program Manager, Project Coordinator, or another closely related project management role.
- Experience coordinating schedules, timelines, procurement, staffing, and budgets across technical or cross-functional projects.
- Ability to commit 20+ hours per week.
- Strong written English communication skills.
