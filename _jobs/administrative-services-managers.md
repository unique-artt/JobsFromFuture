**About the Role**

Experienced Administrative Services Managers are being hired to help evaluate and improve cutting-edge AI systems. In this role, you'll evaluate AI-generated administrative operations content and workflows, leveraging your real-world expertise in planning, directing, and coordinating an organization's supportive services, such as facilities, records management, procurement, and office operations.

**Key Responsibilities**

- Evaluate AI-generated office operations plans, facilities and vendor management documents, records-management procedures, and other administrative artifacts for quality and accuracy.
- Compare AI-generated responses head-to-head based on operational soundness, policy compliance, and cost-effectiveness.
- Review administrative scenarios involving budgeting, procurement, space planning, support-services staffing, and process improvement and provide expert feedback.

**Required Qualifications**

- 4+ years of experience as an Administrative Services Manager, Office Manager, Facilities Manager, Administrative Director, Business Operations Manager, or another closely related administrative management role.
- Experience planning, directing, or coordinating an organization's supportive services, such as recordkeeping, mail distribution, facilities planning and maintenance, procurement, and other office support services.
- Ability to commit 20+ hours per week.
- Strong written English communication skills.
