**Role Overview**

A leading AI research lab is collaborating on a project to advance frontier medical problem-solving in oncology. Oncologists based in the United States with hands-on clinical practice experience are being sought. The goal of this project is to identify gaps in AI model reasoning around real-world oncology cases, particularly in clinical trial design, patient eligibility, and treatment decision-making, and to support the training of cutting-edge AI models.

This is a short-term, high-impact, remote opportunity for oncologists excited about applying their clinical expertise to frontier AI research, with the possibility of extension based on performance.

**Key Responsibilities**

- Create realistic, nuanced oncology tasks and scenarios, particularly around clinical trials, that expose weaknesses in frontier AI model responses
- Evaluate AI-generated clinical reasoning for accuracy, completeness, and alignment with current standard-of-care guidelines
- Identify flawed logic, missed diagnoses, incomplete treatment rationale, or unsafe recommendations in model outputs
- Provide clear, well-structured expert responses and chain-of-thought reasoning that reflect real clinical decision-making

**Qualifications**

You are a strong fit for this project if you meet most or all of the following:

- MD or DO who is currently pursuing or has completed a fellowship in medical oncology or hematology/oncology
- Currently practicing in the United States and seeing oncology patients, or has done so within the past three years (3+ years of post-residency experience strongly preferred)
- Ability to articulate clinical reasoning clearly and precisely in written form

You will be engaged as an independent contractor. This is a fully remote role that can be completed on your own schedule. Projects can be extended, shortened, or concluded early depending on needs and performance. Payments are weekly based on services rendered. No H1-B or STEM OPT support is available at this time.
