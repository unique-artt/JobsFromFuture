The research teams behind the best-known AI models need software engineering judgment they can't generate on their own. Your standards and expertise become the rubric that frontier AI is graded against.

This is a standing listing for engineers who want AI training and evaluation work, not a specific job opening. It stays open whether or not a matching project is running this week. New projects start on short notice, so instead of posting and pulling a listing each time, one stays open per field and is drawn from when a project begins.

**What the work looks like**

Engineers in the network are matched to projects like:

- Writing an engineering problem from a system you have shipped, with the requirements, the constraints, and the failure modes, plus the reference answer a model's attempt gets graded against
- Grading model-written code and design: whether it handles the edge cases, whether the complexity is justified, whether the test actually exercises the behaviour it claims to
- Flagging where a model's code passes review and would fail in production, given concurrency, scale, error handling, or what it costs to operate

Software engineering listings here have posted at **$70 to $150 an hour**, set per project by scope and depth.

**What we're looking for**

- Professional experience building and shipping software
- Clear written communication, since most of the work involves explaining your reasoning
- Comfort with ambiguous tasks, and willingness to flag unclear instructions

**What this covers**

This pool spans software engineering, including:

- Frontend (React, TypeScript, Next.js); backend (Java, Go, Node.js); full-stack
- Mobile (Android, iOS); embedded and IoT
- DevOps and platform engineering; systems and performance engineering
- QA and automation; security engineering

Where you trained matters less than whether you have owned code in production and dealt with what it did there.

**How it works**

Apply here with your resume and confirm where you work from, then complete a short interview, about 20 minutes total.

Your background is verified and you're added to the software engineering pool. When a project needs your stack or specialty, matching people from the pool are invited to that specific listing. It names the rate, the hours, and the client, and any hiring decision is made there.

**What to expect**

This listing does not accept or reject candidates, so you will not get a decision on it. You'll be reached out to when a project matches your background. Depending on what clients are running, that can be within a week or several months.

Finishing the domain expert interview and joining every network you qualify for both improve your odds of being matched. You only need to sign up once to be eligible across all of them.

You will be engaged as an independent contractor. This is a fully remote role that can be completed on your own schedule. Payments are weekly based on services rendered.
