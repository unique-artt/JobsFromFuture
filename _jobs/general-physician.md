Experienced Medical Practitioners (MDs and DOs) are being hired to evaluate health AI models in partnership with a leading AI lab. You will assess model outputs across diagnosis, clinical reasoning, and care workflows.

**Key Responsibilities**

- Evaluate AI model outputs for clinical accuracy, safety, and alignment with medical standards
- Score diagnoses, case reasoning, and care recommendations against expert judgment
- Write structured feedback on clarity, safety, and clinical rigor
- Flag failure modes that compromise patient safety
- Collaborate asynchronously with AI researchers to improve model performance

**Required Qualifications**

- MD or DO from an accredited medical school
- Active, unrestricted US medical license
- Residency complete
- Currently practicing and seeing patients
- 3+ years of post-residency experience in your current role strongly preferred
- Practice in primary care, internal medicine, and hospital-based specialties (Primary Care, General Internal Medicine, Family Medicine, Emergency Medicine, Critical Care)
- Sharp attention to detail and clear written communication
- Located in the US

**Project Timeline**

- Start Date: Immediate
- Duration: 6 weeks
- Commitment: Part-time, 20+ hours per week

**Application & Onboarding**

- Upload your resume and application form
- Complete a short conversational interview on your background, experience, and motivations
- Credential verification: license status, residency completion, and current practice setting
- Final roster placement is confirmed with the project team before your start date
- Receive next steps and onboarding details within a few days
