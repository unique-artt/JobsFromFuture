**About the Role**

Experienced Financial Managers are being hired to help evaluate and improve cutting-edge AI systems. In this role, you'll evaluate AI-generated financial management content and workflows, leveraging your real-world expertise in overseeing accounting, banking, credit, and financial planning activities across branches, offices, or departments.

**Key Responsibilities**

- Evaluate AI-generated financial plans, budgets, forecasts, and other financial management artifacts for quality and accuracy.
- Compare AI-generated responses head-to-head based on financial soundness, regulatory compliance, and analytical rigor.
- Review financial scenarios involving cash flow oversight, budgeting, pricing decisions, and financial reporting and provide expert feedback.

**Required Qualifications**

- 4+ years of experience as a Financial Manager, Finance Director, Branch Manager, Financial Planning Manager, Financial Reporting Manager, or another closely related financial management role.
- Experience overseeing accounting, banking, credit, insurance, securities, or financial planning activities at the branch, office, or department level.
- Ability to commit 20+ hours per week.
- Strong written English communication skills.
