**About the Role**

Experienced Property, Real Estate, and Community Association Managers are being hired to help evaluate and improve cutting-edge AI systems. In this role, you'll evaluate AI-generated property management content and workflows, leveraging your real-world expertise in operating and maintaining residential, commercial, and industrial properties and managing community associations.

**Key Responsibilities**

- Evaluate AI-generated property operating budgets, lease analyses, maintenance plans, association governance documents, and other property management artifacts for quality and accuracy.
- Compare AI-generated responses head-to-head based on financial soundness, regulatory and lease compliance, and operational feasibility.
- Review scenarios involving tenant and owner relations, rent collection, vendor contracts, capital improvements, and association board matters and provide expert feedback.

**Required Qualifications**

- 4+ years of experience as a Property Manager, Real Estate Manager, Community Association Manager, HOA Manager, Leasing Manager, or another closely related property or association management role.
- Experience planning, directing, or coordinating the leasing, operation, maintenance, or governance of residential, commercial, or industrial real estate properties or community associations.
- Ability to commit 20+ hours per week.
- Strong written English communication skills.
