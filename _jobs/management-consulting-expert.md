The research teams behind the best-known AI models need management consulting judgment they can't generate on their own. Your standards and expertise become the rubric that frontier AI is graded against.

This is a standing listing for consultants who want AI training and evaluation work, not a specific job opening. It stays open whether or not a matching project is running this week. New projects start on short notice, so instead of posting and pulling a listing each time, one stays open per field and is drawn from when a project begins.

**What the work looks like**

Consultants in the network are matched to projects like:

- Writing a case prompt from an engagement you ran, with the client situation, the data, and the constraint, plus the reference answer a model's attempt gets graded against
- Grading model-built market sizings and profitability analyses: whether the segmentation is sensible, whether the driver tree holds, whether the recommendation follows from the numbers
- Flagging where a model produces a clean-looking answer that would not survive a partner review or a client steering committee

Past rates have run **$90 to $120 an hour**, set per project by scope and depth.

**What we're looking for**

- Professional experience in strategy, operations, or transformation consulting
- Clear written communication, since most of the work involves explaining your reasoning
- Comfort with ambiguous tasks, and willingness to flag unclear instructions

**Where people in this pool have worked**

The most common backgrounds, though the pool is broader than this:

- MBB and the strategy arms of the Big Four
- Boutique and tier-two strategy firms
- Functional specialists: pricing, operations, org design, post-merger integration
- In-house strategy, corporate development, or internal consulting at a large company

The firm name matters less than whether you owned the analysis and the client-facing recommendation, not just the slide production.

**How it works**

Apply here with your resume and confirm where you work from, then complete a short interview, about 20 minutes total.

Your background is verified and you're added to the management consulting pool. When a project needs management consulting expertise, matching people from the pool are invited to that specific listing. It names the rate, the hours, and the client, and any hiring decision is made there.

**What to expect**

This listing does not accept or reject candidates, so you will not get a decision on it. You'll be reached out to when a project matches your background. Depending on what clients are running, that can be within a week or several months.

Finishing the domain expert interview and joining every network you qualify for both improve your odds of being matched. You only need to sign up once to be eligible across all of them.

You will be engaged as an independent contractor. This is a fully remote role that can be completed on your own schedule. Payments are weekly based on services rendered. No H1-B or STEM OPT support is available at this time. This listing is for a role at an external company.
