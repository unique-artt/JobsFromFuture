**About the project**

Help evaluate AI assistants on realistic everyday tasks. You give an AI assistant a task like one from your own life or work, work with it over several turns, and rate the result against a professional standard.

**What you will do**

- Write short, realistic requests, like a real user would type.
- Work with the assistant on the task: steer it, answer its questions, and push back when it is wrong.
- Rate the experience and the deliverable, and write short notes on what went well or badly.

**Who we are looking for**

- You use AI chat assistants often for everyday personal and work questions, and you write clearly.
- No minimum years of experience.
- You use AI assistants at least occasionally.
- You write clearly in English.

**Details**

- Remote, about 10 hours per week, flexible schedule.
- Pay: $30/hour.
- Only open to candidates in countries where the assistant is available.

You will be engaged as an independent contractor. This is a fully remote role that can be completed on your own schedule.
