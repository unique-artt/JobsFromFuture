The research teams behind the best-known AI models need materials science judgment they can't generate on their own. Your standards and expertise become the rubric that frontier AI is graded against.

This is a standing listing for materials scientists who want AI training and evaluation work, not a specific job opening. It stays open whether or not a matching project is running this week. New projects start on short notice, so instead of posting and pulling a listing each time, one stays open per field and is drawn from when a project begins.

**What the work looks like**

Materials scientists in the network are matched to projects like:

- Writing a materials problem from work you have done, with the system, the processing history, and the property target, plus the reference answer a model's attempt gets graded against
- Grading model-written structure-property reasoning: whether the microstructure explains the property, whether the characterization supports the claim, whether the processing window is realistic
- Flagging where a model's proposed material meets the spec on paper and fails in service, given fatigue, corrosion, thermal cycling, or what can actually be manufactured

Past rates have run **$60 to $120 an hour**, set per project by scope and depth.

**What we're looking for**

- A graduate degree in materials science or a related field, with research or industrial experience
- Clear written communication, since most of the work involves explaining your reasoning
- Comfort with ambiguous tasks, and willingness to flag unclear instructions

Where you trained matters less than whether you processed and characterized the material yourself, recently enough to explain the calls you made.

**How it works**

Apply here with your resume and confirm where you work from, then complete a short domain interview, about 20 minutes total.

Your background is verified and you're added to the materials science pool. When a project needs materials science expertise, matching people from the pool are invited to that specific listing. It names the rate, the hours, and the client, and any hiring decision is made there.

**What to expect**

This listing does not accept or reject candidates, so you will not get a decision on it. You'll be reached out to when a project matches your background. Depending on what clients are running, that can be within a week or several months.

Finishing the domain expert interview and joining every network you qualify for both improve your odds of being matched. You only need to sign up once to be eligible across all of them.

You will be engaged as an independent contractor. This is a fully remote role that can be completed on your own schedule. Payments are weekly based on services rendered. No H1-B or STEM OPT support is available at this time.
