**Location:** Remote (United Kingdom)

**Overview**

Join a leading AI lab's cutting-edge GenAI team and help build foundational AI models from the ground up. Molecular Biology subject-matter experts (SMEs) are being sought who have designed DNA and RNA sequences themselves, including primers, plasmids, guide RNAs, mRNA constructs and repair templates, and who can bring that depth to the quality of AI training data.

This is a W-2 employment position with Cincinnatus LLC (or appropriate international entity), with the opportunity to be placed at a leading AI Lab as part of their extended workforce.

**Key Responsibilities**

- Work with research and engineering teams to find where a model's molecular biology reasoning breaks down, and help close those gaps.
- Design hard, domain-relevant tasks and write the accurate, well documented solutions that become ground truth. That spans primers for PCR, qPCR and cloning, plasmids and expression vectors, gRNA and sgRNA for CRISPR editing, mRNA and RNA constructs, and HDR or repair templates.
- Review molecular biology tasks and model outputs against expert-quality solutions, and give clear written feedback on correctness, rigor and biological reasoning.
- Write the guidelines and rubrics used to judge sequence design quality, covering guide and target selection, homology arm length, primer melting temperature and specificity, codon optimization, and regulatory elements.
- Work alongside other subject matter experts so the training data stays consistent and accurate.

**Core Qualifications**

- An advanced degree, PhD strongly preferred, in molecular biology, genetics, biochemistry, synthetic biology, bioengineering or a related life science field.
- Hands-on experience designing nucleic acid constructs before the experiment runs, across several of primers, plasmids and vectors, gRNA and sgRNA, mRNA, and HDR or repair templates, together with the cloning strategy behind them (Gibson, Golden Gate, Gateway, restriction) and codon optimization.
- A strong peer-reviewed publication record, weighted toward first-author work in notable venues such as Nature and the Nature family, Cell, eLife, Nucleic Acids Research, PNAS and EMBO Journal. Please include links to your publications when you apply.
- Clear career progression.
- You can commit reliably to at least 20 hours a week on weekdays.
- Previous work in AI training, model evaluation or data annotation is a plus.
- Strong written communication, and the ability to explain the reasoning behind a design choice clearly and precisely.
