The research teams behind the best-known AI models need investment management judgment they can't generate on their own. Your standards and expertise become the rubric that frontier AI is graded against.

This is a standing listing for asset and wealth managers who want AI training and evaluation work, not a specific job opening. It stays open whether or not a matching project is running this week. New projects start on short notice, so instead of posting and pulling a listing each time, one stays open per field and is drawn from when a project begins.

**What the work looks like**

Asset and wealth managers in the network are matched to projects like:

- Writing an investment case or a client scenario from your own mandate or book, with the thesis, the constraints, and the risk budget, plus the reference answer a model's attempt gets graded against
- Grading model-built portfolio construction, allocation, and tax strategy: whether the factor exposures are intended, whether the benchmark is the right one, whether the withdrawal sequencing holds, whether the attribution actually explains the return
- Flagging where a model's recommendation is defensible on paper and would breach a real mandate or fail a real client, given liquidity, concentration limits, drawdown tolerance, or time horizon

Comparable investment management projects have posted at **$100 to $130 an hour**, set per project by scope and depth.

**What we're looking for**

- Professional experience in portfolio management, buy-side research, multi-asset allocation, financial planning, or private client advisory
- Clear written communication, since most of the work involves explaining your reasoning
- Comfort with ambiguous tasks, and willingness to flag unclear instructions

**Where people in this pool have worked**

The most common backgrounds, though the pool is broader than this:

- Long-only asset managers and mutual fund complexes
- Hedge funds and multi-strategy platforms
- Pension, endowment, and sovereign allocators
- Private banks and wirehouse wealth management
- Registered investment advisors and multi-family offices
- Trust, estate, and fiduciary practices

The firm name matters less than whether you made the calls yourself, recently enough to explain the tradeoffs behind them.

**How it works**

Apply here with your resume and confirm where you work from, then complete a short interview, about 20 minutes total.

Your background is verified and you're added to the asset and wealth management pool. When a project needs asset or wealth management expertise, matching people from the pool are invited to that specific listing. It names the rate, the hours, and the client, and any hiring decision is made there.

**What to expect**

This listing does not accept or reject candidates, so you will not get a decision on it. You'll be reached out to when a project matches your background. Depending on what clients are running, that can be within a week or several months.

Finishing the domain expert interview and joining every network you qualify for both improve your odds of being matched. You only need to sign up once to be eligible across all of them.

You will be engaged as an independent contractor. This is a fully remote role that can be completed on your own schedule. Payments are weekly based on services rendered.
