Board-certified general surgeons (MDs and DOs) are being hired to evaluate health AI models in partnership with a leading AI lab.

You will read de-identified patient charts and judge AI answers to clinical questions in general surgery.

**Key Responsibilities**

- Evaluate AI answers to general surgery questions for clinical accuracy, safety and alignment with current standards of care
- Check each answer against the patient chart and decide which of two responses is stronger
- Write structured rationales on clinical rigor, completeness and clarity
- Flag errors that could compromise patient safety

**Required Qualifications**

- MD or DO from an accredited medical school
- Board certified in ABS General Surgery through an ABMS member board or the AOA
- Active, unrestricted US medical license
- Residency complete, plus fellowship where your specialty requires one
- Currently practicing and seeing patients in the US, with at least 1 year of independent practice after training
- 3+ years of post-training experience strongly preferred
- Clear written communication and close attention to detail

**Project Details**

- Remote and hourly, with flexible part-time hours
- Start date: immediate

**Application & Onboarding**

- Upload your resume
- Complete a short conversational interview on your background, experience and motivations
- Credential verification: license status, board certification, training and current practice setting
- Receive next steps and onboarding details within a few days

You will be engaged as an independent contractor. This is a fully remote role that can be completed on your own schedule.
