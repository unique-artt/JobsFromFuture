Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about finding and applying for roles on Jobs From Future.

What is Jobs From Future?

Jobs From Future is a curated job board for remote, on-site, contract, and task-based roles from leading companies and AI research labs. Every listing is structured so you can see the pay, requirements, and location before you apply.

Are the jobs on this site remote?

Most roles are fully remote. Some are hybrid or on-site, and those are clearly labelled with their location on the job card and job page. Use the "Remote" tab to see remote-only roles.

What is a task-based role?

Task-based roles pay a flat fee for a defined piece of work — for example, annotating a dataset or completing a research task — rather than an hourly wage. The total pay is shown on the job card.

How do I apply?

Click "Apply Now" on any job page. You will be taken directly to the company's application page to complete your application. Jobs From Future is a listing platform, not the employer.

Does Jobs From Future hire me?

No. We list opportunities from external companies and platforms. The employer or platform you apply through handles hiring, contracts, and payment. We do not make hiring decisions.

Is there a fee to use this site?

No. Browsing and applying through Jobs From Future is free for job seekers. We may earn a commission when you apply through some links, at no extra cost to you.

How often are new jobs added?

New roles are added regularly. Check back often, or use the tabs to filter by Remote, Tasks, Contracts, or Full-time to see what is currently available.

Can I post a job on Jobs From Future?

Not yet. Job posting for employers is planned for a future release. For now, the board is curated directly by our team.
